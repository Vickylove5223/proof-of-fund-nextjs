Many Nigerian students aiming for a Canadian study permit in 2026 are asking about the Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) option. While the Student Direct Stream (SDS) has ended, a GIC remains one of the strongest and most trusted forms of proof of funds recognized by IRCC. In this guide, we explain the current Canada GIC requirements for Nigerian applicants, how it works, the benefits compared to regular bank statements, and practical ways to meet this requirement even if you don’t have the full amount readily available in liquid cash.

### What is a GIC and Why Does IRCC Accept It?

A Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) is a secure investment product offered by participating Canadian banks. You deposit a lump sum, and the bank guarantees your principal plus interest. For study permit purposes, it proves you have dedicated funds specifically set aside for living expenses in Canada. IRCC views GIC as highly reliable because the money is held in Canada, reducing doubts about accessibility or source of funds. Even though it is no longer mandatory, officers often look favorably on applications that include a GIC. ![](http://afrikamombraids.local/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2d287143-7207-4096-9a11-be7c49121ca6-200x300.webp)

### Current Canada GIC / Proof of Funds Requirements 2026

For study permit applications submitted (outside Quebec):

*   **Living expenses (single student)**: CAD $22,895 per year
*   **First-year tuition**: As stated in your Letter of Acceptance (LOA)
*   **Return transportation**: Approximately CAD $1,000 – $2,000

**Total typical requirement for a single student**: CAD $42,000 – $55,000+ depending on your program’s tuition. **GIC-Specific Amount**: Most students purchase a GIC covering at least the living expenses portion (CAD $22,895). You can combine GIC with other proofs such as tuition payment receipts, sponsor letters, or Nigerian bank statements. **Family Members**: Add approximately CAD $5,607 – $6,170 for each additional family member.

### Is GIC Mandatory for Nigerian Students ?

No. The SDS program, which made GIC compulsory for certain nationalities, ended in November 2024. However, for Nigerian applicants facing higher scrutiny, including a GIC significantly strengthens your application and helps address concerns about ties to Nigeria and fund accessibility.

### Participating Canadian Banks for GIC

Popular institutions that offer student GIC programs include:

*   Royal Bank of Canada (RBC)
*   Scotiabank
*   Bank of Montreal (BMO)
*   TD Canada Trust
*   CIBC

These banks issue official GIC certificates that you can upload with your study permit application.

### Challenges for Nigerian Students Opening a GIC

Opening a GIC directly from Nigeria can be difficult because:

*   Most Canadian banks require the student to be in Canada or have specific international transfer processes.
*   High forex rates and transfer limits from Nigerian banks.
*   Delays in international wire transfers and documentation.

Many students therefore combine a Nigerian bank Proof of Funds with other supporting documents or explore compliant funding options.

### Strong Alternatives and Compliant Solutions for Nigerians

If transferring funds for a full GIC is challenging, you can still meet IRCC expectations using:

*   4–6 months bank statements from CBN-licensed Nigerian banks (Access, GTB, Zenith, UBA, etc.) showing sufficient balance.
*   Sponsor affidavits with supporting financial documents.
*   Education loans or combination of sources.
