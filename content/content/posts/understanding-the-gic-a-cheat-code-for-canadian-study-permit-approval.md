One of the most common reasons for a Canadian study permit refusal is "finances." Often, visa officers from countries like Nigeria are skeptical about the availability and source of funds in a standard bank statement.

To solve this, IRCC recommends the **Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC)**. At **POF NG**, we advocate for the GIC because it provides the ultimate proof that your living expenses are already in Canada and ready to be used. If you don't have the personal funds to secure a GIC, we can provide the necessary support at an affordable rate.

### **What is a GIC and How Does It Work?**

The GIC is a program where you transfer your living expenses for the first year (currently **$20,635 CAD**) to a Canadian bank before you even apply for your visa.

*   **Fund Security:** Once you arrive in Canada, the bank gives you an initial lump sum (usually 30% of the total).
    
*   **Monthly Stipend:** The remaining balance is paid out to you in equal installments over the next 11 months to cover your rent, food, and other bills.
    

For Nigerians, **CIBC** is the preferred bank because it is widely available and tailored for international students from Africa.

### **3 Easy Steps to Secure Your GIC**

#### **Step 1: Application and Registration**

Visit the CIBC International Student Pay portal. You will need to provide your student information, upload your international passport, and use your Letter of Acceptance (LOA) for address verification.

*   **Pro Tip:** Choose to pay in **US Dollars** if you are paying from a Nigerian Domiciliary account.
    

#### **Step 2: Payment and Wire Transfer**

Once your application is approved, CIBC will send you an invoice. You will need to do a wire transfer from your dollar account.

*   **Money Laundering Compliance:** Most Nigerian banks cannot send $15,000+ USD in a single transaction. It is often necessary to break the payment into two installments (e.g., $10,000 and $5,000) to ensure it is processed smoothly.
    

#### **Step 3: Download Your Letters**

Once the bank receives your funds, they will issue two critical documents:

1.  **Welcome Letter**
    
2.  **Investment Letter**
    
    These documents are your "golden tickets." Include them in your visa application to show the consular officer that your funds are verified and held in a Canadian institution.
    

### **Why Choose a GIC Over a Standard Statement?**

A GIC eliminates the "lack of trust" factor. It shows the visa officer that your money is not just "resting" in a Nigerian account to be withdrawn later—it is already in Canada. This significantly maximizes your chances of success, especially for Study Permit applications.
