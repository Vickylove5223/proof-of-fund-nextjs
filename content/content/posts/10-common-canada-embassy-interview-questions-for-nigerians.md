1\. Why do you want to go to Canada?

------------------------------------

This is often the opening question. The visa officer wants to understand your primary motivation.

Is it for tourism, education, or work? Your answer should be clear, specific, and align perfectly with the visa category you applied for.

**How to Answer:** Be direct and honest. If you're a student, talk about the quality of education in Canada and why it's the right fit for your career goals. If you're a tourist, mention specific landmarks you want to see. Avoid vague answers like "for a better life." For official information, always refer to the [Government of Canada's immigration website](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html).

2\. Why did you choose this particular university/course?

(For Students)
------------------------------------------------------------------------

This question tests how much research you've done. The officer wants to see that your decision is well-thought-out, not random.

They are looking for genuine academic intent.

**How to Answer:** Mention specific aspects of the university or program that attracted you. This could be a particular professor, the course curriculum, research facilities, or the school's reputation in your chosen field. Show that you have a clear academic plan. Websites like [EduCanada](https://www.educanada.ca/) can be a great resource for this research.

3\. Who is sponsoring your trip?

--------------------------------

The visa officer needs to be certain that your trip is financially supported by a reliable source. This could be yourself, your parents, a spouse, or another close relative.

**How to Answer:** State clearly who your sponsor is and your relationship to them. Be prepared to provide details about their occupation and financial capacity. Honesty and consistency with your application documents are crucial here.

5\. What are your ties to Nigeria?

----------------------------------

The officer needs to be sure that you will return to Nigeria after your authorized stay ends. Strong ties to your home country are the best evidence of this.

**How to Answer:** Talk about your family, property you own, a job you will return to, or business investments in Nigeria. The more significant your responsibilities and connections are at home, the more believable your intent to return becomes.

6\. Have you traveled outside Nigeria before?

---------------------------------------------

Your travel history can work in your favor. It shows that you have traveled to other countries and respected their immigration laws by returning home within your permitted time.

**How to Answer:** Be truthful. If you have traveled, mention the countries and the purpose of your visits. If you haven't, simply say so. A lack of travel history is not an automatic cause for refusal, especially for first-time student applicants.

7\. What are your plans after you complete your studies/visa expires?

---------------------------------------------------------------------

This is another question designed to assess your intent to return to Nigeria. Your answer must show that you plan to use your Canadian experience or education back home.

**How to Answer:** Connect your Canadian plans to your future in Nigeria. For example: "After completing my Master's in Data Science, I plan to return to Nigeria to work for a leading tech firm in Lagos, as there is a high demand for these skills."

8\. Do you have any relatives in Canada?

----------------------------------------

It is vital to be honest here. Immigration officials have access to extensive records and can easily verify this information.

Lying can lead to an automatic refusal and a potential ban.

**How to Answer:** If you have relatives in Canada, disclose their name, relationship to you, and their status in Canada (e.g., citizen, permanent resident, student). Having relatives there is not necessarily a negative factor.

9\. What does your sponsor do for a living?

-------------------------------------------

This question verifies the legitimacy of your financial support. The visa officer wants to ensure your sponsor has a stable and legal source of income sufficient to cover your expenses.

**How to Answer:** Clearly state your sponsor's job title, the company they work for, and a brief description of their role. This should match the information in their employment letter and financial documents. You can stay updated on travel news via sources like [The Guardian Nigeria](https://guardian.ng/).
