Planning to study, work, or immigrate abroad is an exciting journey. As you prepare your documents, one of the most critical requirements you'll encounter is the Proof of Funds (POF). This document proves to embassies and schools that you have the financial capacity to support yourself. But a common question we hear is: what does it actually cost to arrange for a POF in Nigeria?

Understanding the costs involved, often referred to as an "interest rate" or service fee, is key to budgeting for your application. The rates can vary significantly depending on the financial institution and the service provider you choose.

The most common Proof of Funds interest rate in Nigeria typically ranges from **2.5% to 4.2%** of the total amount required. More competitive banks like Globus, Fidelity, and Stanbic often charge between 2.5% and 3.5%, while other major banks may charge from 3.5% upwards for their service.

What Exactly is a POF "Interest Rate"?
--------------------------------------

First, let's clarify a key point. When we talk about an "interest rate" for Proof of Funds, it's not a loan interest. A Proof of Funds service is not a loan. Instead, the rate is a service fee or commission you pay to a financial partner for temporarily placing the required funds in an account for verification purposes. This fee covers the administrative costs and the service of making the funds available for your visa or school application.

This service allows you to meet official requirements without having to liquidate your assets or tie up a large sum of your own money for an extended period. You simply pay a small percentage of the total fund amount.

A Breakdown of Typical Interest Rate Tiers in Nigeria
-----------------------------------------------------

In Nigeria, the financial market for POF services is competitive, leading to different pricing tiers. Knowing these can help you find a service that fits your budget.

### The More Affordable Range: 2.5% to 3.5%

Some of the most cost-effective rates are offered by a group of customer-focused banks. Institutions like Globus, Parallax, Fidelity, FCMB, and Stanbic typically charge within this range. Their competitive pricing makes them a popular choice for students and individuals looking for affordable and reliable POF services. Choosing one of these can significantly reduce your application costs.

### The Standard Range: 3.5% to 4.2% and Higher

More established and popular banks like First Bank, Access Bank, and UBA often have service fees that start from 3.5% and can go up to 4.2% or slightly higher. While the cost is more, many applicants trust these institutions due to their long-standing reputation. The choice between these tiers often comes down to a balance of cost and personal banking preference. Factors That Influence Your POF Rate
------------------------------------

The rate you are quoted isn't arbitrary. Several factors determine the final percentage you will pay for your Proof of Funds service.

*   **The Amount Required:** The total value of the funds you need to show is the primary factor. The service fee is a direct percentage of this amount.
*   **The Duration:** How long do the funds need to remain in the account? Most visa applications, like for the [UK student visa](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money), require funds to be held for at least 28 days. Longer durations may sometimes attract a slightly higher fee.
