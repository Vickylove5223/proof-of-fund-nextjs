### Step 1: Identify the Purpose of Your POF

Before anything else, you need to clearly define **why you need proof of funds**. Different purposes have different requirements, and understanding your specific need will guide the entire process. Common POF purposes include:

*   **Education** — [Student visa applications](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/money), tuition payment proof, or study abroad requirements
*   **Tourist Travel** — International tourism visa applications showing financial capacity
*   **Business Trip** — [Business visa applications](https://www.gov.uk/standard-visitor/visit-on-business) or proof of funds for international meetings
*   **Business Contracts within Nigeria** — Local tender applications, project bids, or supplier agreements

Each purpose may have specific documentation standards and financial thresholds, so it's crucial to be clear about your exact need from the start.

### Step 2: Determine How Much You Need

Once you've identified your purpose, the next step is to determine **the exact amount required** for your proof of funds. This amount is usually specified by:

*   **Educational institutions** (schools, colleges, universities)
*   **Embassies or consulates** (for visa applications)
*   **Travel companies** (for tour packages)
*   **Contract issuers** (for business bids and tenders)
*   **Immigration authorities** (for residency or travel permits)

Make sure you confirm the exact amount needed before proceeding. Having insufficient funds in your statement can lead to rejection or delays.

### Step 6: Make Payment for the First Month's Interest

Once we receive your documents and confirm everything is in order, we'll send you our **company account details** for payment. You'll need to pay the **first month's interest fee** before your account is funded. This payment ensures that the POF amount is credited into your account on time. **Renewal and Extensions** If you need to hold the funds beyond the initial month, you'll pay the interest fee again at the beginning of each renewal period. We'll notify you **two days before your tenor expires**, so you have time to decide whether to extend or let it mature.

### Step 7: Your Account is Funded with a Mandate (Lien) Placed

After your payment is confirmed, the agreed POF amount will be **transferred into your bank account as real money**. This is not a fake balance — it's an actual credit that will appear on your [bank statement](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/b/bank-statement.asp). **Important: Funds are Earmarked (Blocked)** Once the funds are credited, a **mandate (lien)** is placed on the account. This means: ✅ The funds **will show in your account balance** ✅ The funds **will appear on your bank statement** ❌ You **cannot withdraw** the exact POF amount during the agreed tenor However, if it's your salary or business account, you can still:

*   Receive other deposits
*   Make transfers
*   Use your debit card
*   Pay bills
*   Conduct normal banking transactions

Only the specific POF amount is blocked from withdrawal.

### Step 8: Understanding Transaction Narrations

When the POF funds are credited to your account, the transaction will include a **narration** that explains the source of the funds. These narrations are important for credibility and transparency. Common acceptable narrations include:

1.  Liquidation of Investment
2.  Investment Proceeds
3.  Liquidation of Assets
4.  Dividend Proceeds
5.  Salary
6.  Sales or Purchase of Property/Land
7.  Payment of Goods
8.  Gift from \[Name\] to \[Name\]
9.  Educational Funds/Sponsorship
10.  IFO Customer's Name
11.  Assets Proceeds
12.  Rent
13.  Other legitimate business or personal transactions

These narrations help to ensure that your bank statement looks authentic and professionally documented.

### Step 9: Activate Your Account (For New Bank Accounts)

If you opened a **new bank account** for your POF, it's important to activate it before the funds are credited. Simply **transfer a small amount** (e.g., ₦1000 - ₦5000) into the account to test that it's active and working properly. This ensures there are no issues when the POF amount is deposited.

### Step 10: Maturity and Withdrawal of Funds

On the agreed **maturity date**, the POF amount will be **automatically withdrawn** from your account by our partner bank. At this point, you can:

*   Generate your bank statement directly from your mobile banking app
*   Visit your bank branch to request an official stamped statement

The statement will show the full period during which the funds were present in your account — this becomes your **verifiable proof of funds** to present to embassies, schools, contract issuers, or any other institution.
