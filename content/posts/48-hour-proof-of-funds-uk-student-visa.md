### Required Maintenance Funds and Tuition Calculations

Specifically, UKVI requires students to show proof of funds for UK student visa applications covering:

*   **Tuition fees:** Full amount for the first academic year or remaining course duration
*   **Living costs:** £1,334 per month for London-based institutions
*   **Living costs:** £1,023 per month for institutions outside London
*   **Additional expenses:** Accommodation deposits and course materials

Furthermore, these calculations must reflect current exchange rates and include any pre-paid amounts. Consequently, accurate financial planning becomes essential for visa success.

### Acceptable Document Types and Standards

UKVI accepts specific document types as valid proof of funds for UK student visa applications:

*   **Bank statements:** Official statements showing transaction history
*   **Bank letters:** Formal confirmation from financial institutions
*   **Building society statements:** Recognized savings account documentation
*   **Official financial sponsorship:** Government or institutional funding letters

Moreover, all documents must be in English or accompanied by certified translations. Additionally, electronic statements require official bank letterheads and authentication stamps.

### Critical Timing Requirements

The UKVI maintains strict timing requirements that significantly impact application success:

*   **28-day holding period:** Funds must remain in accounts for 28 consecutive days
*   **31-day recency rule:** Documents must be dated within 31 days of application submission
*   **Continuous availability:** No significant withdrawals during the holding period

Therefore, timing coordination becomes crucial for meeting these overlapping requirements. Furthermore, any gaps in documentation can result in immediate visa rejection.
