### Can I Go to the UK Without IELTS on a Study Visa?
Yes, absolutely! The United Kingdom is one of the easiest top-tier countries for Nigerians to access without an IELTS score. Most UK universities will waive the IELTS requirement if you have a WAEC or NECO certificate with a C6 or higher in English Language. Once the university issues your CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) without requiring IELTS, the UKVI visa officer will not ask for it either.

### Which European Country Gives a Student Visa Easily Without IELTS?
If you are asking, "Can I study in Europe without an IELTS score?", the answer is highly favorable in Eastern and Central Europe. 

*   Poland & Hungary: These countries are incredibly welcoming to Nigerian students. Many universities accept a simple "Medium of Instruction" (MOI) letter from your previous Nigerian university stating that your Bachelor's degree was taught in English.
*   Ireland: As a native English-speaking country in Europe, Ireland accepts WAEC English (C6 or higher) just like the UK, making the visa process extremely straightforward.

### Which Country Gives the Fastest Student Visa?
When it comes to speed, the United Kingdom wins by a landslide, provided you pay for the "Priority Visa" service. While standard processing takes 3 weeks, Priority Service can yield a visa decision in just 5 working days. 

If you are looking at mainland Europe and asking "Which European country is easy to travel to from Nigeria?", countries like Malta, Lithuania, and Latvia have relatively straightforward visa processes, though their processing times can hover around 4 to 8 weeks.
