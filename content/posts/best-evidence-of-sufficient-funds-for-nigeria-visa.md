What Exactly is Proof of Funds (POF)? -------------------------------------

Proof of Funds is an official document, typically a bank statement or a letter from a financial institution, that certifies you have the required financial resources for a specific purpose. Whether you're applying for a student visa to Canada, a tourist visa to the UK, or immigrating to another country, the embassy needs assurance that you won't become a financial burden on their country.

Think of it as your financial resume. It proves your stability and ability to cover expenses like tuition, accommodation, and daily living costs.

It’s important to remember that Proof of Funds is not a loan; it's a way to show that the required money is accessible to you.

The Gold Standard: Your Bank Statement
--------------------------------------

The most widely accepted and best evidence of sufficient funds is a detailed bank statement. This isn't just a simple printout of your balance.

A proper POF bank statement should be official and contain specific information that visa officers look for.

Embassies scrutinize bank statements to see where the money came from. Sudden, large deposits without a clear explanation can raise red flags.

Legitimate sources of funds include:

*   Salary
*   Liquidation of investments or assets
*   Dividends or investment proceeds
*   Proceeds from the sale of property
*   Official gifts from family (often requiring a gift deed)
*   Educational funds or sponsorship
