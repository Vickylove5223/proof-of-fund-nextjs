Every japa conversation in 2026 starts with the same complaint: the UK cut work visas by 90%, and the US is slower and stricter than ever. But migration doesn't shrink — it redirects. While the traditional doors narrowed, a set of newer routes genuinely got easier for Nigerians.

Here are the ten most workable, ranked — with the number that matters most on this site: **how much money each route requires you to show.**

## The Rankings

### 1. Germany — Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)

A points-based **job seeker visa**: no job offer needed. Show qualifications, language basics, and funds — typically a blocked account of about **€1,091/month (~€13,092 for a year)**. Germany's demand for engineers, IT, and health workers keeps absorption high. Students use the similar [blocked-account route](/proof-of-funds-germany-student-visa-nigeria).

### 2. Canada — Category-Based Express Entry

Canada now runs targeted draws (healthcare, trades, French speakers) with lower score cut-offs than general draws. Settlement funds: **CAD $15,263** for a single applicant — full table in our [Canada 2026 amounts guide](/canada-proof-of-funds-2026-new-amounts-in-naira-study-permit-express-entry).

### 3. Portugal — Job Seeker Visa

120 days (extendable) to find work in Portugal, converting to a work permit on hire. Funds: around **3× monthly minimum wage (~€2,610)** plus return ticket — one of Europe's lowest bars. Students compare with the [Portugal student route](/proof-of-funds-for-portugal-student-visa-from-nigeria-2026-complete-guide).

### 4. Finland — Fast-Track Work and Study

Finland processes specialist work permits in as little as two weeks and actively recruits health workers. Students show about **€800/month (€9,600/year)** — see our [Nordic countries guide](/proof-of-funds-for-nordic-countries-student-visas-from-nigeria).

### 5. New Zealand — Green List Roles

Straight-to-residence pathways for listed occupations (nurses, engineers, ICT). Workers with offers don't show POF; accompanying reality: high salaries, small quotas. Students show **NZD $20,000/year**.

### 6. United Arab Emirates — Job Seeker Visa

Dubai's job seeker visa (60–120 days) requires modest bank evidence and no sponsor. It's a launchpad, not a destination — pair with our [Dubai/UAE proof of funds guide](/proof-of-funds-for-dubai-uae-visa-from-nigeria).

### 7. Netherlands — Orientation Year for Graduates

Nigerians who complete a masters anywhere in the world's top-200 universities within three years can get a **one-year Dutch job-search visa** — funds requirement is minimal. The student route feeding it is in our [Netherlands guide](/proof-of-funds-for-netherlands-student-visa-from-nigeria).

### 8. Poland & Hungary — Central European Study Routes

Low tuition, low POF (**€6,000–€9,000 total**), high admission rates — the budget path into the EU. Details in [countries with the lowest proof of funds requirements](/countries-with-the-lowest-proof-of-funds-requirements-for-student-visas-2026).

### 9. Ireland — Critical Skills Employment Permit

English-speaking, EU, tech and pharma demand, and a two-year path to long-term residence. Students need about **€10,000** shown — see the [Ireland student guide](/proof-of-funds-for-ireland-student-visa-from-nigeria-2026-complete-requirements-guide).

### 10. Rwanda & Kenya — African Mobility

Visa-free or visa-on-arrival for Nigerians, growing tech scenes, and no POF hurdles — increasingly real options for remote workers and entrepreneurs building capital before a bigger move.
