### Top Countries You Can Visit (Exemptions)

If you hold a valid, multiple-entry US (B1/B2), UK (Standard Visitor), or Canadian (Visitor) visa, you can often enter the following countries without needing a separate embassy appointment:

#### 1. The Americas & Caribbean
*   Mexico: Any Nigerian with a valid US, UK, Canadian, or [Schengen visa](/schengen-visa-bank-statement-requirements-from-nigeria-6-months) can visit Mexico visa-free for tourism.
*   Costa Rica: Open to those with a multiple-entry US or Canadian visa.
*   Panama: Grants access if you have a US, UK, Canadian, or EU visa that has been used at least once.
*   Belize: Visa-free for US multiple-entry visa holders.
*   Dominican Republic: Open to US, UK, Canadian, and Schengen visa holders.

#### 2. Europe & The Middle East
*   Turkey: While not entirely visa-free, holding a US, UK, or Schengen visa allows Nigerians to easily apply for an instant e-Visa to Turkey online, skipping the rigorous physical embassy process.
*   Georgia: Grants entry to holders of valid US, UK, and Schengen visas.
*   Albania, Montenegro, and Serbia: Often allow entry to those possessing valid UK or US visas.

#### 3. Asia
*   Philippines: Allows a 14-day visa-free entry for Nigerians holding a valid US, Japanese, Australian, Canadian, or Schengen visa.
*   South Korea: Allows transit without a visa if you are traveling to or from the US, Canada, Australia, or New Zealand and hold their visa.
