### 1. Germany (The Best "Free" Tuition Option)
If you are asking, "Which country is free for students?" or "Where is it free to study abroad?", Germany is your best answer. Most public universities in Germany charge absolutely zero tuition fees, even for international students! You only pay a small semester contribution (around €250 - €300).
*   The Catch: While tuition is free, you must open a German Blocked Account and deposit roughly €11,208 to prove you can cover your living expenses. 

### 2. Poland (The Affordable European Hub)
If you want to study in Europe with incredibly low living costs, Poland is fantastic. Tuition fees for international students range from €2,000 to €4,000 per year, and living expenses are among the cheapest in the European Union.

### 3. Malaysia (The Asian Tech & Business Hub)
For those asking "How can I study abroad with the lowest cost?" without worrying about harsh European winters, Malaysia is a goldmine. Universities offer UK and US-accredited degrees for as low as $3,000 to $5,000 per year, and the cost of living in Kuala Lumpur is highly affordable for Nigerians.

### 4. Norway & Finland (Low-Cost Nordic Excellence)
While Norway recently introduced tuition fees for non-EU students, there are still massive scholarship opportunities, and PhD programs are entirely free. Finland offers generous scholarships that often cover 50% to 100% of tuition if you maintain good grades.

### How to Get Free Study Abroad (Scholarships)
If you are searching for "Which country gives a full free scholarship?", you should look into government-funded programs rather than specific countries. Programs like the Chevening Scholarship (UK), Erasmus Mundus (Europe), and DAAD (Germany) offer fully-funded opportunities covering tuition, flights, and living expenses.

### The Hidden Hurdle: Proof of Funds

Here is the biggest secret in international education: Even if you find a country with 100% free tuition, the embassy will still demand Proof of Funds.

Why? Because the host country wants to guarantee you will not become a homeless burden on their economy. You still need to show a bank statement proving you can afford food, rent, and transportation.
