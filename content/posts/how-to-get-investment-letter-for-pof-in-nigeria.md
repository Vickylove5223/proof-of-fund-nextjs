What Exactly is an Investment Certificate/Letter for Proof of Funds? --------------------------------------------------------------------

An Investment Letter is an official document issued by a licensed financial institution. It confirms that you hold investments—such as stocks, bonds, or fixed deposits—of a specific value.

This letter serves as concrete evidence to an embassy or school that you have sufficient, legitimate financial resources that can be liquidated to cover your tuition, living expenses, and travel costs.

Unlike a simple account balance, an investment letter demonstrates long-term financial stability and a credible source of wealth, which visa officers often view more favorably. It shows that the funds weren't just deposited temporarily for the application.
