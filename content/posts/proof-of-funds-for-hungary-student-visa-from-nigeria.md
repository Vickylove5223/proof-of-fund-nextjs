### How Much is the Proof of Funds for Hungary?

The Hungarian Embassy is extremely strict about international students being able to support themselves without relying on public funds. To secure your visa, your bank statement must reflect two major costs:

1.  First Year Tuition: You must show the exact outstanding balance of your first year's tuition (as stated on your admission letter). If you have paid in full, you must provide the official receipt.
2.  Living Expenses: As of the latest 2026 requirements, a single student must show a minimum of €7,000 (approx. ₦12 Million) specifically for living expenses.

The embassy will not accept just any financial document. To avoid rejection, you must provide:
