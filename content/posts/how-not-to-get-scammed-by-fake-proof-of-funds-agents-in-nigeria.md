### The "PDF Editor" Scam (The Most Common Trap)

The most prevalent scam in Nigeria is the PDF Edit. These fraudulent agents will ask for your basic details, charge you a ridiculously low fee (like ₦50,000 for a ₦30 Million statement), and disappear for a few hours. 

They will then return with a PDF bank statement that has your name on it. What they have done is taken an old bank statement, used Adobe Photoshop or a PDF editor, and manually typed your name and account details over the original. 

Why it destroys you: Embassies do not just glance at the PDF. They email or use secure portals to verify the account balance directly with the issuing bank. The bank will immediately confirm that the account does not exist or that the balance is false. Your visa will be denied, and you will be banned.

### How to Spot a Fake POF Agent

If you want to protect your relocation dreams, look out for these massive red flags:

1. They Refuse Physical Meetings: Scammers hide behind WhatsApp numbers. They will always have an excuse as to why they cannot meet you in a physical office.
2. They Promise "Non-Verifiable" Cheap Rates: If an agent tells you "the embassy won't check this one," run away immediately. Every single POF statement can and will be checked.
3. They Don't Deposit Real Cash: A legitimate POF company deposits actual liquid cash into your bank account. A scammer only hands you a piece of paper.
