### The 2026 Nairaland Visa Processing Timeline

Based on hundreds of successful "Visa Alerts" posted by Nigerian students, this is the expected timeline for 2026:

#### 1. Conditional Offer & Deposit (1 - 4 Weeks)
After applying to the university, you receive a conditional offer. You must pay your required tuition deposit to secure your spot.

#### 2. The Proof of Funds Waiting Period (28 Days)
This is where 80% of applicants get stuck. Before your university issues your CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies), many require you to prove you have your maintenance funds ready. Furthermore, the UKVI legally requires these funds to sit untouched in your account for 28 consecutive days. If you don't have the money ready immediately, your entire timeline is delayed by a month.

#### 3. CAS Issuance (1 - 2 Weeks)
Once you pass the university's internal financial checks and pre-CAS interviews, they issue your CAS number.

#### 4. Visa Application & Biometrics (1 Week)
You fill out the UKVI application online, pay the IHS (Immigration Health Surcharge) and visa fees, and book your biometric appointment at TLScontact in Ikeja, Victoria Island, or Abuja.

*   Priority Service: Nairaland users report decisions in roughly 5 to 7 working days.
*   Standard Service: Typically takes 15 to 21 working days, barring any "Not Straightforward" (NSF) delays.
