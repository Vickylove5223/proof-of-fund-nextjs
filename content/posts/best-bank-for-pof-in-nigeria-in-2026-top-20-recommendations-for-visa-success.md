Immigration officers look for credibility, stability, and easy accessibility of funds. Tier-1 commercial banks with strong international reputation perform better because:

*   Microfinance banks (OPay, Kuda, Moniepoint, PalmPay, EDFIN, etc.) — Often flagged or viewed with suspicion by major embassies.
*   Fintech-only accounts without full commercial banking license.
*   Lesser-known or newly licensed banks with limited track record.

Always confirm with your specific visa type and destination.
