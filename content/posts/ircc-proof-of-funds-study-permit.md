### How Much Does the IRCC Require for a Study Permit?

The IRCC calculation is straightforward but expensive. To get your study permit approved, your bank statement must show a balance that covers:

1.  First Year of Tuition: The exact amount stated on your Letter of Acceptance (LOA) from your Canadian Designated Learning Institution (DLI).
2.  Living Expenses: As of the recent policy updates, a single student must show a minimum of $20,635 CAD (approx. ₦24 Million, depending on exchange rates) specifically for living expenses. 
3.  Travel Costs: A buffer for your round-trip flight (typically estimated at $2,000 to $3,000 CAD).

*Note: If you are bringing family members, you must add an additional $5,055 CAD for the first family member, and $5,893 CAD for each subsequent family member.*

### What Does the IRCC Accept as Proof?

The IRCC is incredibly strict about the format of your financial evidence. They will accept:
*   A verifiable bank statement spanning the past 4 to 6 months.
*   A Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC) from a participating Canadian financial institution.
*   Proof of a student/education loan from a recognized financial institution.
*   A bank draft that can be converted to Canadian dollars.

The most common and flexible method for Nigerians is a 4-to-6-month bank statement from a Tier-1 Commercial Bank (such as Zenith, Globus, or Parallex).
