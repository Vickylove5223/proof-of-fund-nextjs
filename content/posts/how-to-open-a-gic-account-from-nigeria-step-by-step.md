You already know a GIC strengthens a Canada study permit application — our guides cover [why the GIC is a cheat code for approval](/understanding-the-gic-a-cheat-code-for-canadian-study-permit-approval) and the [overall requirements from Nigeria](/canada-gic-proof-of-funds-nigeria-requirements-everything-you-need-to-know). What nobody explains properly is the **mechanics**: which bank, which forms, how the money actually leaves Nigeria, and how long each step takes.

This is that guide.

## Step 0: Know the Number

For 2026 applications outside Quebec, the GIC amount tracks the living-cost requirement — **CAD $22,895** (Scotiabank quotes the required deposit plus a ~$200 program fee). At current rates budget **₦27m–₦30m** for the wire, plus transfer charges. Confirm the live figure on your chosen bank's page before wiring.

## Step 1: Choose Your Bank

| Bank | Application | Notes for Nigerians |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scotiabank (StartRight)** | Online portal | The most-used option; investment account opened before arrival; [program page](https://startright.scotiabank.com/ca/en/international-student/student-gic.html) |
| **RBC** | Online | Accepts funding up to CAD $75,000 — useful if you want extra settlement funds in the same wire; [program page](https://www.rbcroyalbank.com/en-ca/new-to-canada/international-student-gic-program/) |
| **CIBC** | Online, digital tracking | No processing fee on FX; transfers up to CAD $50,000; [program page](https://www.cibc.com/en/special-offers/international-student-gic.html) |

All three are IRCC-recognised. Choose on practicalities: where your Canadian city has branches, and whose portal your agent/university recommends.

## Step 2: Apply Online and Get Your Account Details

Complete the bank's GIC application with your passport and admission letter. On approval (usually days), the bank opens an investment account and emails **wire transfer instructions** with your unique account/reference number. Guard that document — payments without the reference get stuck.

## Step 3: The Nigerian Leg — Wiring the Money

This is where timelines slip. To send CAD abroad from Nigeria you'll go through your bank's international transfer desk:

1. **Fund your naira account** with the full equivalent plus fees and a rate buffer
2. **Complete Form A** (the CBN's application for foreign exchange) through your bank — supporting documents: passport, admission letter, the GIC instruction letter
3. The bank sources FX and wires CAD to your GIC account via SWIFT
4. **Timeline: 3–10 working days** in normal conditions; FX availability can stretch it — start at least a month before you need the certificate

Practical notes: wire from an account [with clean, documented history](/funds-parking-why-large-unexplained-deposits-get-nigerian-visas-refused) — the source of your GIC money can come up in your visa review; and confirm whether your bank quotes all-in fees or deducts along the SWIFT chain (short-paying the GIC by $50 delays your certificate).

## Step 4: Receive Your GIC Certificate

Once funds land, the bank issues your **GIC Investment Directive/Certificate** (typically 1–5 business days). That document goes into your study permit application as proof of the living-cost component — alongside tuition payment evidence and the rest of your [Canada student file](/proof-of-funds-for-canada-student-visa-from-nigeria-complete-guide).

## Step 5: After Arrival

Visit a branch in Canada with your passport and study permit to activate your account. You receive an initial lump sum (around $2,000–$4,000 depending on bank), then **monthly or bi-monthly payouts** over 12 months — your living allowance.

## If Your Visa Is Refused

The GIC is fully refundable on refusal: submit the refund request with your refusal letter, and funds return to the originating account, typically within **4–8 weeks**. Full details in [do I get my POF money back if refused](/do-i-get-my-pof-money-back-if-my-visa-is-refused).
