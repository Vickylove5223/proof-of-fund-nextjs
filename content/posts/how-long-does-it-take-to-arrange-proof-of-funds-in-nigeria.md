The most common WhatsApp message we receive: *"My appointment is on [date] — can you arrange proof of funds in time?"*

Here is the honest answer, in two parts. **The funding itself: 24–48 hours.** Placing legitimate funds in a top Nigerian bank with statements and letters is fast. **The history your embassy expects: that's the real clock** — and it ranges from zero days to six months depending on where you're applying.

This guide gives you the true timeline per destination, so you can plan backwards from your deadline.

## Timeline by Destination

| Destination | History expected | Realistic minimum runway |
| --- | --- | --- |
| UK (student/worker) | 28 consecutive days | **~5 weeks** (28 days + issuance + buffer) |
| Canada (study permit) | 4–6 months preferred | **6 weeks – 6 months** (GIC shortcuts part of it) |
| USA (F1/B1/B2) | 6-month statements reviewed, no seasoning rule | **Days–weeks** (documentation quality matters most) |
| Schengen | 3–6 months of statements | **3–6 months** ([the new 6-month rule](/schengen-visa-bank-statement-requirements-from-nigeria-6-months)) |
| Australia (student) | ~3 months preferred | **6–12 weeks** |
| Transfer-based (Germany, Norway, [Netherlands](/proof-of-funds-for-netherlands-student-visa-from-nigeria)) | None — money must arrive | **2–6 weeks** (wire + FX processing) |

## What Each Stage Actually Takes

1. **Consultation and structuring — same day.** Destination, amount (check yours on the [calculator](/proof-of-fund-calculator)), account holder rules ([who may hold funds varies by country](/who-can-sponsor-your-uk-student-visa-proof-of-funds)).
2. **Funding placement — 24–48 hours** into GTB, Zenith, Access, or UBA under lien.
3. **Seasoning — the embassy's number, not ours.** 28 days for the UK is non-negotiable; a single day short restarts the clock ([the 28-day traps](/common-mistakes-in-28-day-proof-of-funds-for-uk-visas-and-how-to-avoid-them)).
4. **Documentation — 1–3 days.** Stamped statements and attestation letters ([costs and branch mechanics here](/bank-statement-and-reference-letter-costs-in-nigeria-gtb-access-zenith-uba)).
5. **Hold through processing — weeks to months.** Funds must stay in place; embassies [re-verify balances after submission](/how-do-embassies-verify-bank-statements-in-nigeria).

## "My Deadline Is Too Close" — Real Options

*   **UK in under 28 days?** The maintenance clock can't compress — but check whether your sponsor can certify maintenance (workers) or whether your CAS date can shift. A [48-hour UK arrangement](/48-hour-proof-of-funds-uk-student-visa) covers the funding; the calendar covers the rest.
*   **Canada in weeks, not months?** The [GIC route](/how-to-open-a-gic-account-from-nigeria-step-by-step) substitutes a transfer for months of history on the living-cost component.
*   **USA next week?** Achievable — US interviews weigh [current documented funds and consistency](/us-b1-b2-visitor-visa-bank-statement-requirements-from-nigeria) over seasoning.
*   **Schengen without 6 months of history?** Move your travel date, or apply through a consulate still accepting 3 months. Do not reach for [backdated statements — here's exactly why](/backdated-bank-statement-for-visa-in-nigeria-why-it-fails-and-what-works).
