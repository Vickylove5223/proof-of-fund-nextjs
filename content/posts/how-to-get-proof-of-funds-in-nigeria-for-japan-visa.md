Understanding Japan's Proof of Funds Requirement
------------------------------------------------

Proof of Funds is an official bank document that proves you can financially support yourself during your visit to Japan. It's not a loan; it's evidence of your financial stability.

The Japanese Embassy needs this assurance to grant you a visa.

The embassy doesn't state a fixed minimum amount, as the required sum depends on your trip's length, your planned activities, and your accommodation style. Your POF must convincingly show that you can handle all expenses without needing to work illegally or overstaying your visa.

How Much Money Do You Need for a Japan Visa? --------------------------------------------

While there's no magic number, we can create a reasonable estimate. A good rule of thumb is to budget for three main things: flights, accommodation, and daily living expenses.

### Estimating Your Trip Costs

*   **Return Flights:** A round-trip ticket from Nigeria to Japan can range from ¥200,000 to ¥400,000 (Japanese Yen), depending on the airline and time of booking.
*   **Accommodation:** A budget-friendly hotel could cost around ¥10,000 per night. For a 10-day trip, that's ¥100,000.
*   **Daily Expenses:** For food, local transport, and sightseeing, a moderate budget is about ¥15,000 per day. For a 10-day trip, this amounts to ¥150,000.

**Total Estimated Cost (10-day trip):** ¥200,000 (flights) + ¥100,000 (hotel) + ¥150,000 (daily expenses) = **¥450,000**. It's wise to have more than the estimated amount to show a healthy financial buffer. Aiming for ¥600,000 to ¥800,000 would be a strong position.

### Converting to Nigerian Naira

Using our higher estimate of ¥800,000:

**800,000 JPY x 9.5 NGN = ₦7,600,000**

5 million to ₦8 million in your bank account for a 10-day tourist trip would be a solid Proof of Funds for your Japan visa application.
