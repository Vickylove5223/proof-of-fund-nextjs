### What is the 28-Day Rule?

The UKVI dictates that the required proof of funds (which covers your outstanding tuition fees plus 9 months of living expenses) must sit in your bank account for a minimum of 28 consecutive days before you submit your visa application.

Here is the critical breakdown of how the 28-day rule works:

1. The Balance Cannot Drop: Your bank account balance must not drop below the required visa amount for even a single day during that 28-day period. If it drops by even ₦1,000 on day 15, the 28-day clock restarts from zero.
2. The 31-Day Printing Rule: Your bank statement must be printed no more than 31 days before the date you submit your visa application online and pay your visa fee. 
3. The Date of Application: The 28-day period must be completed *before* the date you pay for your visa application online, not the date of your biometric appointment.

### How much proof of funds do I need for a UK student visa?

For a UK Student Visa, your POF is calculated as:
Outstanding Tuition Fees + Living Expenses (for up to 9 months)

*   If studying in London: £1,334 per month (approx. £12,006 total for living expenses).
*   If studying outside London: £1,023 per month (approx. £9,207 total for living expenses).
