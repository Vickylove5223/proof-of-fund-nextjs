Key Pathways to JAPA to Ireland from Nigeria
--------------------------------------------

There are two primary routes for Nigerians looking to relocate to Ireland: studying and working. Let's explore what each path entails.

### The Study Visa Route

This is one of the most popular ways to move to Ireland. The process involves getting admission into a recognized Irish higher education institution.

Once you have your acceptance letter, you can apply for a student visa.

A major part of the student visa application is proving you have enough money to support yourself. You must show that you have access to at least €10,000 per year for living expenses, in addition to the full tuition fees for your first year of study.

### The Work Visa Route (Critical Skills Employment Permit)

If you are a professional with skills that are in high demand in Ireland, the Critical Skills Employment Permit is your golden ticket. The Irish government maintains a list of eligible occupations in sectors like IT, engineering, healthcare, and finance.

To qualify, you need a job offer from an Irish employer for a role on this list, with a minimum annual salary of €32,000 (for most roles). This permit allows you to work in Ireland and eventually apply for long-term residency.

The Irish embassy needs to see that you can fund your stay without becoming a burden on the state. This is done through a document called Proof of Funds (POF).
