### The IRCC "Liquidity" Rule

The golden rule of Proof of Funds for Canada is that the money must be unencumbered and immediately accessible (liquid). 

The IRCC needs to know that the moment you land in Toronto, Vancouver, or Calgary, you can easily withdraw that money to pay for rent, food, and tuition. 

### Why Fixed Deposits Often Fail

When you present a standard Fixed Deposit Certificate, you are showing the embassy that your money is "locked away" for a specific tenure (e.g., 6 months or 1 year) to earn interest. 

If your fixed deposit maturity date is *after* your intended travel date, the visa officer will argue that you cannot access the money when you actually need it, leading to a refusal.

How to make a Fixed Deposit work (The Hard Way):
If you insist on using a fixed deposit, you must obtain a specialized letter from your bank manager explicitly stating that the fixed deposit can be liquidated at any time without restriction (even if it means losing the accrued interest). Without this specific encumbrance-free wording, the IRCC will likely reject it.
