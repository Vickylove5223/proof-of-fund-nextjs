### How Much Money Do You Need?

According to [Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)](https://www.canada.ca/), you need to show you have enough money for:

*   Your first year of tuition fees.
*   Living expenses for one year (CAD $20,635 for a single applicant, as of January 1, 2024).
*   Travel expenses to and from Canada.

The living expense amount is fixed by IRCC, but your tuition will vary depending on your school and program. Always check your admission letter for the exact tuition fee.

What Documents Are Accepted as Proof of Funds? ----------------------------------------------

The Canadian embassy is very specific about the documents they accept. Simply having the money isn’t enough; you need the right paperwork to prove it.

Here are the most common forms of [acceptable proof of funds](/guides/what-is-acceptable-proof-of-funds):

*   **Bank Statements:** Your personal bank account statements for the past four to six months.
*   **Guaranteed Investment Certificate (GIC):** A GIC from a participating Canadian financial institution is a highly recommended method.
*   **Sponsor’s Financial Documents:** If a family member is sponsoring you, you’ll need their bank statements, a letter of sponsorship, and proof of their relationship to you.
*   **Proof of a Student Loan:** An official letter from a bank confirming your approved student loan.
*   **Investment Statements:** Documents showing stocks, bonds, or other investments that can be easily liquidated.
