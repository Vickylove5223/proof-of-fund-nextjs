### What Real Estate Valuation Actually Does (Home Ties)

When you submit a property valuation report, a Deed of Assignment, or a Certificate of Occupancy (C of O), you are proving to the visa officer that you have Strong Home Ties. 

Home ties are reasons why you will return to Nigeria after your studies or visit. A visa officer looks at your property and thinks: *"This applicant owns a ₦100 Million house in Lagos; they are highly likely to return home."* This is excellent for your application, but it does not count towards your Proof of Funds.

### Why Property Fails as Proof of Funds

Proof of Funds (POF) must be 100% liquid and unencumbered. 

The embassy needs to know that the moment you land in their country, you can immediately access the money to pay for rent, groceries, and tuition. You cannot instantly pay a Canadian landlord with a plot of land in Abuja. Selling real estate takes months, meaning the asset is illiquid. 

If you rely solely on property valuation as your POF, your visa will be refused under the grounds of "insufficient liquid funds."
