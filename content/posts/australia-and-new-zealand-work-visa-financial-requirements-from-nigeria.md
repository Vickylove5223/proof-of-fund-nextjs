Here's a surprise for applicants coming from UK-style rules: **Australia's main skilled visas have no formal proof-of-funds requirement, and neither does New Zealand's employer-sponsored work visa.** No £1,270-style maintenance test, no 28-day rule.

But before you close the tab: money still decides outcomes on both routes — through state-nomination settlement funds, genuine-capacity checks, and the brutal arithmetic of landing in Sydney or Auckland with empty pockets. Here's the real financial picture for Nigerians in 2026.

## Australia: Where Money Enters the Skilled Routes

### Subclass 482 (Skills in Demand) — employer-sponsored

No POF requirement. Your employer's nomination and your salary meeting the temporary skilled income threshold do the financial work. Your bank statement only surfaces if the visa officer doubts the genuineness of the arrangement.

### Subclass 189/190/491 — points-based skilled visas

The federal 189 has no funds requirement. **State nominations (190/491) are different**: several states require a **declaration of settlement funds** — commonly **AUD $20,000 – $35,000** for a single applicant, more with family. Some states take declarations at face value; others can request evidence. Treat the declared figure as money you should genuinely be able to show — [in verifiable, documented form](/proof-of-funds-vs-bank-statement-vs-bank-reference-letter).

### The student comparison

If you're weighing work against study routes: the student visa DOES have a hard requirement — **AUD $29,710** in living costs plus tuition — covered in our [Australia student visa guide](/australia-student-visa-proof-of-funds-from-nigeria-2026-complete-requirements-guide).

## New Zealand: The AEWV and Green List

### Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV)

With a job offer from an accredited employer, there's no POF test — accreditation and the wage threshold carry the file. 

### Green List — Straight to Residence

Nurses, doctors, engineers, and ICT specialists on the Green List get direct residence pathways with a job offer. Again no POF — the constraint is landing the offer from Nigeria, which usually means registration first (NZ nursing council, engineering accreditation).

### Students and partners

NZ student visas require **NZD $20,000/year** in living costs. Partners of workers may need to show support funds where they're not working — check current settings on [Immigration New Zealand](https://www.immigration.govt.nz/).

## So Where Do Nigerians Actually Need Money?

1. **State-nomination settlement declarations** (AUD $20k–$35k) — the closest thing to POF on these routes
2. **Skills assessments, English tests, registrations** — the pre-visa pipeline costs ₦2m–₦6m before any visa is filed
3. **Landing funds** — no rule requires them, but 2–3 months of living costs (AUD/NZD $8,000–$15,000) is the difference between job-hunting calmly and desperately
4. **Family files** — partners and children multiply both declared funds and real costs

And when any of that money moves through your accounts before an application, the usual rules apply: [clean history, documented sources](/funds-parking-why-large-unexplained-deposits-get-nigerian-visas-refused), banks that [verify cleanly](/how-do-embassies-verify-bank-statements-in-nigeria).
