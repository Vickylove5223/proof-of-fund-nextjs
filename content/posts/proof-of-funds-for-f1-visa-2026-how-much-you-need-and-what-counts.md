### **What Exactly is Proof of Funds (POF)?**

Proof of Funds is a set of legal documents you present during your F1 visa application and to your university to obtain your **I-20 form**. These documents prove that you can cover:

*   **Tuition and fees** for one year.

*   **Living expenses** for one year.

*   **Miscellaneous costs**, including books, health insurance, and transportation.

The total amount varies by school, so it is crucial to check your school’s **Cost of Attendance (COA)** or the deficit listed on your I-20.

### **How Much Do You Need to Show?**

For the I-20 and the visa interview, you are generally required to show enough liquid funds to cover the **first year of your studies**. For example, if your university estimates $25,000 for tuition, $12,000 for living expenses, and $3,000 for extras, you must show a total of \*\*$40,000\*\*.

While you only show funds for the first year, be prepared to explain to the Visa Officer how you plan to cover subsequent years of your program.

### **What Counts as Acceptable Proof of Funds?**

To satisfy the requirements, you can use one or a combination of the following:

*   **Bank Statements:** Savings or checking accounts showing a clear balance.

*   **Scholarship/Sponsorship Letters:** Official documents from an organization or the school.

*   **Educational Loans:** Approval letters from a bank or recognized body.

*   **Sponsor Letters:** If a sibling or parent is sponsoring you, they must provide a signed letter and their bank statements.

### **Expert Tips for a Successful Application**

1.  **Be Recent:** Ensure all bank statements and documents are from the last **3 to 6 months**.

2.  **Match Your I-20:** Whatever you tell the Visa Officer must exactly match the information on your I-20 form.

3.  **Originality Matters:** Never forge documents. Schools and embassies can verify these, and forgery results in an automatic flag or ban.

4.  **Blue Pen for Signatures:** Using a blue pen for signatures helps prove the document is an original and not a duplicate.
