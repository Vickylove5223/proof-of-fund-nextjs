### Are There Banks Not Accepted by UKVI in Nigeria?

Historically, the UKVI published a strict "banned list" of Nigerian banks they refused to accept due to high rates of fraud. While that specific public blacklist is no longer actively published, their internal policy remains incredibly strict: The UKVI will only accept statements from financial institutions that are fully regulated by the CBN and capable of satisfactory international verification.

This means that while a bank might not be explicitly "banned," it can still be deemed unacceptable by the visa officer reviewing your case. 

### Does UKVI Accept Microfinance Banks?

Technically, some large, CBN-licensed microfinance banks are accepted, but in reality, using a microfinance bank for UKVI is a massive risk. 

Here is why UK visa officers frequently reject microfinance statements:
1. Verification Failures: When the UK embassy emails a microfinance bank to verify a statement, they often receive no reply, or the reply takes weeks. If the UKVI cannot verify the funds, they refuse the visa.
2. Credibility Issues: Visa officers know that many online POF scammers use obscure microfinance banks to generate fake PDF statements. Consequently, they view almost all microfinance statements with extreme suspicion.

### Beware of Digital Wallets & Cooperative Societies

You must absolutely avoid using the following for your Proof of Funds:
*   Digital-Only Wallets / FinTech Apps: Unless they hold a full commercial banking license, the UKVI will reject statements from mobile money apps or pure fintech wallets.
*   Cooperative Societies: Funds held in community cooperative societies or credit unions are rarely accepted because they lack stringent international banking regulations.
