### What Makes a Bank "Approved" by the UKVI?

For a bank to be accepted, it must meet these core criteria:
1. It must be regulated by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN).
2. It must keep electronic records that the UKVI can verify.
3. The funds must be held in your name (or a legal sponsor's name).
4. The bank must be willing to respond to embassy verification emails promptly.
