### Why Microfinance Banks Are Risky for Proof of Funds

Most users on platforms like Nairaland and Quora agree that microfinance institutions face severe recognition issues with international immigration offices. Here is why many embassies don't recognize microfinance banks as reliable:

1. Verification Limits: Embassies often have a hard time verifying records with microfinance banks. If an immigration officer cannot reach the bank to confirm your statement, your visa will likely be denied.
2. Capacity Doubt: Smaller banks raise instant red flags when there are sudden, large deposits. Embassies often view this with a "perception of limited financial capacity."
3. Format Issues: Microfinance statements often lack standardized international clearing details, SWIFT codes, or the professional formatting required by strict immigration offices.
