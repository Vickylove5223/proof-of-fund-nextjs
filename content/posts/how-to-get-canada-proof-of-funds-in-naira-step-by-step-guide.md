Common Mistakes to Avoid When Preparing Your POF
------------------------------------------------

A small mistake in your POF documentation can lead to a visa rejection. Here are a few common pitfalls to avoid:

*   **Using an Outdated Exchange Rate:** Always use the most current rate available on the day you finalize your documents.
*   **Sudden Large Deposits:** A large, unexplained deposit right before you generate your bank statement can raise red flags. This is known as "fund parking" and is often scrutinized.
*   **Not Meeting the Duration Requirement:** Some visa streams require the funds to be in your account for a specific period (e.g., 28 days for the UK). Ensure you meet this requirement.
*   **Forgetting the Buffer:** Failing to account for currency fluctuations can leave you short of the required amount if the Naira weakens.
