### **Top Bank Statement Mistakes to Avoid**

#### **1\. Presenting Fake Bank Documents**

This is the fastest way to get a permanent ban from countries like the UK. Embassies frequently contact banks and even the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) to verify signatures, mandate cards, and account history. If they find any discrepancies, your application is finished.

#### **2\. The "Lump Sum" Deposit Red Flag**

Sudden, large deposits (lump sums) appearing in your account a few months before your application are a major red flag. If your regular transactions are around 200k–500k and you suddenly deposit 5 million, it looks dodgy. If you have a legitimate lump sum, you **must** justify it with receipts, invoices, or a Letter of Explanation in your cover letter.

#### **3\. Pulling Out Funds Too Early**

Never withdraw your funds until you have received a final decision from the embassy. Some applicants borrow money to show for the application and return it 2–3 weeks later, thinking the decision has been made. If the officer checks your account on the day they review your file and finds the money gone, your visa will be denied for "insufficient funds."

#### **4\. Inconsistent Spending Patterns**

If you claim to have dependents but your bank statement shows your salary sitting untouched with zero withdrawals for everyday needs, it looks like you are misrepresenting your lifestyle. A natural bank statement should show a pattern of regular income and regular expenses (rent, groceries, school fees).

#### **5\. Business Money vs. Personal Money**

For entrepreneurs, a huge balance in a corporate bank account does not automatically count as your money. You must include a letter on the organization's letterhead stating that the business is sponsoring your trip and that you have sole authority over those funds.

### **How to Build a "Believable" Bank Statement**

*   **Consistency is Key:** A stable pattern of income and expenditure is more believable than a sudden high balance.
    
*   **The Cover Letter:** Always include a cover letter to explain any unusual transactions. This answers the officer's questions before they even have a chance to doubt you.
    
*   **Keep a Healthy Buffer:** Your closing balance should ideally be 1.5x to 2x the estimated cost of your trip to show that you won't be broke after the vacation.
