Understanding Canada's Official Proof of Funds Figures
------------------------------------------------------

Proof of Funds is your way of showing the Canadian government that you can financially support yourself and your family upon arrival. These funds are meant to cover your living expenses while you settle in and find a job.

The official figures are published by [IRCC](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship.html) and are updated periodically. It is essential to understand that the figures provided are minimums, and having more funds can strengthen your application.

The amount you need depends entirely on the visa or immigration program you're applying for. Each program has its specific financial requirements, so be sure to verify the exact amount needed for your situation.

### Proof of Funds for a Canadian Study Permit

IRCC more than doubled the cost-of-living financial requirement for study permit applicants, effective September 2025. A single applicant must now show they have **CAD $22,895** in addition to their first year's tuition fees and travel costs.

This increase reflects the rising cost of living in Canada and ensures that students can adequately support themselves during their studies.

**Sample Calculation (Student):**

*   First Year's Tuition: CAD $18,000
*   Cost-of-Living Requirement: CAD $22,895
*   **Total POF Required: CAD $40,895**

At an example exchange rate of NGN 1,200 per CAD (please note this rate is for illustrative purposes and changes daily), the total amount in Naira would be approximately **₦49,000,000**. Always use the most current exchange rate when calculating your POF in Naira.

### Proof of Funds for Skilled Immigrants (Express Entry)

For those applying through the Federal Skilled Worker Program or Federal Skilled Trades Program, the funds required are based on family size. According to the [official IRCC website](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/documents/proof-funds.html), the 2026 minimum amounts are:

| Number of Family Members | Funds Required (in CAD) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | $15,263 |
| 2 | $19,001 |
| 3 | $23,360 |
| 4 | $28,362 |
| 5 | $32,168 |
| 6 | $36,280 |
| 7 or more | $38,771 |

**Sample Calculation (Family of 3):**

A family of three would need to show CAD $23,360. Using the same example exchange rate of NGN 1,200 per CAD, this translates to approximately **₦28,000,000**.

It's advisable to calculate this amount regularly, considering the Naira's fluctuation.

The Challenge of the Fluctuating Naira: A Major Hurdle for Nigerians
--------------------------------------------------------------------

As you can see, the biggest challenge for Nigerian applicants is the volatile exchange rate. The Naira amount you need today could be significantly different next week, making financial planning incredibly difficult.

This is why we always advise our clients to have a buffer of at least 10-15% more than the calculated amount to safeguard against sudden currency depreciation.

Check the daily rate with your financial institution, and the [Bank of Canada](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/rates/exchange/) also provides data on the Canadian dollar's value. Regularly monitoring these resources is crucial for accurate financial planning.
