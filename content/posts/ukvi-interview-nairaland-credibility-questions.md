### Top Financial Questions Asked by UKVI

If you are called for a credibility interview, the visa officer wants to ensure you are a genuine student who has the actual financial means to survive in the UK without working illegally.

#### 1. "Who is funding your studies?"
You must state exactly whose account the bank statement belongs to. If it is your account, say so. If it is your parent's account, confirm their relationship to you. Do not mention third-party uncles, friends, or agencies. The UKVI only accepts statements in your name or your legal parent/guardian's name.

#### 2. "What is your sponsor's occupation and annual income?"
If your parent is sponsoring you, the officer will probe to see if their income logically matches the millions sitting in the bank account. You must know their job title, their business name (if self-employed), and a rough estimate of their annual earnings. 

#### 3. "Where did the sudden lump sum of money in your account come from?"
This is the trickiest question. If a large amount of money was deposited into your account specifically to meet the 28-day rule, you must have a logical explanation. For example:
*   *"My father sold a piece of land to fund my education and deposited the proceeds into my account."*
*   *"I liquidated my business assets and stocks."*
*   *"It was a gift deed from my parents transferred into my personal savings."*

#### 4. "How much are your living expenses in the UK?"
You must know the exact figure required by the UKVI. 
*   London: £1,334 per month (approx. £12,006 for 9 months).
*   Outside London: £1,023 per month (approx. £9,207 for 9 months).
If you cannot recite these numbers, the officer will assume you haven't genuinely planned your finances.
