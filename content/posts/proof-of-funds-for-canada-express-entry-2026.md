### The 2026 Express Entry Fund Requirements

To stay eligible for Canada Express Entry in 2026, you must prove you have enough money to settle in Canada with your family. The exact amount depends entirely on the size of your family (including yourself, your spouse, and dependent children—even if they are not traveling with you).

While the IRCC updates these figures every May, the current baseline requirements (in CAD) are approximately:
*   1 Family Member (Single): ~$14,690 CAD
*   2 Family Members: ~$18,288 CAD
*   3 Family Members: ~$22,483 CAD
*   4 Family Members: ~$27,297 CAD
*   5 Family Members: ~$30,690 CAD
*(Note: Always check the official IRCC website for the exact daily exchange rate and minor annual adjustments).*

### The "Unencumbered" Rule

The most critical rule for Express Entry proof of funds is that the money must be unencumbered. This means the funds must be readily available, liquid cash that is not tied to debt.

What the IRCC will NOT accept for Express Entry:
*   Real estate equity or property valuations.
*   Vehicles or personal assets.
*   Borrowed money from loan sharks or friends.
*   Money locked in inaccessible mutual funds.

What the IRCC DOES accept:
*   Bank statements from a recognized Tier-1 commercial bank showing liquid cash.
*   Official letters from your bank detailing your outstanding debts (credit cards/loans) alongside your liquid cash balance.
