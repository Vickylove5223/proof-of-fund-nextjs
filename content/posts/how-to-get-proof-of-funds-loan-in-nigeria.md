What Exactly is a Proof of Funds Loan? --------------------------------------

It's important to distinguish a Proof of Funds loan from a traditional loan. A traditional loan provides you with cash to spend on personal needs, which you repay over time with interest.

A POF loan, however, is a non-spendable facility. The money is placed in your account solely for verification by an embassy or institution.

Think of it as leasing the funds. You pay a fee to have the money sit in your account for a specific period—usually 30 to 60 days—long enough to satisfy the authorities.

Once your visa or application process is complete, the funds are withdrawn by the finance company. This service is completely legitimate and is a standard practice for many travelers and students.
