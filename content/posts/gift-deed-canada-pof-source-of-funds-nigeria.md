### What is a Gift Deed for Canada POF?

A Gift Deed is a legally binding document that proves the money transferred into your account is a non-repayable gift, not a loan that you have to return after your visa is approved. 

When you use a Gift Deed as a source of funds explanation, you are legally guaranteeing to the Canadian government that the money belongs to you completely and is solely intended for your tuition, living expenses, and travel.

### How to Prepare a Gift Deed in Nigeria

A standard letter typed on your laptop and signed by your uncle is not sufficient for the IRCC. A Gift Deed must carry legal weight. Here is how you prepare one in Nigeria:

1. Drafting: A registered lawyer must draft the Gift Deed, explicitly stating the donor's relationship to you, the exact amount gifted, and a clause confirming the money is unconditionally given without any expectation of repayment.
2. Court Affidavit: You and the donor must take the drafted document to a recognized court in Nigeria (such as a High Court or Magistrate Court) to swear an affidavit. The document is then stamped and signed by a Commissioner for Oaths.
3. Attachment: When submitting your visa application, you must attach this sworn Gift Deed alongside your bank statement.
