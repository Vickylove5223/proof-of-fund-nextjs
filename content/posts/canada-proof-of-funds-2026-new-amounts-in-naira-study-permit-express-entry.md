If you are planning Canada in 2026, throw away every article quoting the old $10,000 requirement. **IRCC has more than doubled the study permit proof of funds to CAD $22,895**, and Express Entry settlement funds have risen again with the annual update. ## Study Permit Proof of Funds 2026

Effective from September 2025 and applying to all 2026 applications, a single applicant (outside Quebec) must show:

*   **CAD $22,895** for first-year living expenses — roughly **₦26m – ₦29m** depending on the exchange rate
*   **PLUS first-year tuition** (typically CAD $15,000 – $35,000)
*   **PLUS travel costs** to and from Canada

In practice, a realistic single-applicant file now shows **₦45m – ₦65m+** in total available funding. Each accompanying family member adds to the living-expense requirement — see our guide on [proof of funds for a Canada student visa with family](/guides/how-to-get-proof-of-funds-for-canada-student-visa-with-family-in-nigeria).

## What Counts as Acceptable Proof

*   Bank statements (6 months) and official bank letters
*   Fixed deposits and verifiable investments
*   GIC certificates — the [GIC remains the "cheat code" for study permit approval](/guides/understanding-the-gic-a-cheat-code-for-canadian-study-permit-approval); Scotiabank, RBC and CIBC all run student GIC programs Nigerians can fund (see the [Canada GIC requirements from Nigeria](/guides/canada-gic-proof-of-funds-nigeria-requirements-everything-you-need-to-know))
*   A sponsor's funds with proper documentation — rules in our [sponsor bank statement guide](/guides/sponsor-bank-statement-for-canada-study-permit-nigeria-complete-requirements-tips)

What does **not** count: money that appeared last week, unverifiable investments, crypto balances, and statements from institutions IRCC can't verify.
