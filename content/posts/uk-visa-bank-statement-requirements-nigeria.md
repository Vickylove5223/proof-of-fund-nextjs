### The Mandatory UKVI Bank Statement Checklist

To ensure your Nigerian bank statement is accepted by the UK embassy, it must explicitly display all of the following:

1. Your Name: The account must be in your name (or your parent/legal guardian's name with supporting birth certificates). Business accounts are generally not accepted unless specifically applying for a business-related visa route.
2. The Account Number: Fully visible and identifiable.
3. The Date of the Statement: The statement must be printed no more than 31 days before the date you submit your visa application online.
4. The Bank's Name and Logo: It must be printed on the bank’s official letterhead.
5. The 28-Day Rule (For Students & Workers): If you are applying for a Student Visa or Skilled Worker Visa, the statement must show that the minimum required funds have been in the account for 28 consecutive days without dropping by even a single Naira.

### Electronic vs. Stamped Bank Statements

Many Nigerian banks now offer digital PDF statements. While the UKVI accepts electronic statements, they strongly recommend that they either:
*   Have a clear, verifiable QR code or digital stamp.
*   Be accompanied by a supporting letter from the bank confirming its authenticity.
*   Be physically stamped on every page by the branch manager if printed in a banking hall.
