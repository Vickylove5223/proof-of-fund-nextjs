The Top 3 Nigerian Banks for Proof of Funds
-------------------------------------------

If you are looking to process a Proof of Funds certificate directly through a commercial bank, these three banks are widely recognized for their processes:

### 1\. Zenith Bank

Zenith Bank is a popular choice for many travelers. Interestingly, you do not always need to have a pre-existing, long-term account with them to initiate this process, though having one helps.

*   **Processing Time:** Typically takes 7 to 12 working days.
*   **Bank Charges:** They generally charge a flat rate of 5% of the total amount requested.
*   **Example:** If your visa requires a Proof of Funds of ₦6,000,000, the bank will charge you roughly ₦300,000 to process the financial backing and certificate.

### 2\. Guaranty Trust Bank (GTBank)

GTBank offers a very accessible Proof of Funds service for prospective international students, tourists, and workers. You can walk into any of their branches nationwide to request a POF for your visa application.

*   **Processing Time:** Usually takes between 3 to 15 working days.
*   **Bank Charges:** Their processing fees range between 4% to 5% of the total funds required.

### 3\. Polaris Bank

Polaris Bank is known for having a very rigid, reliable, and strict Proof of Funds processing system. Because of their thoroughness, their bank statements and POF certificates are highly respected by embassies.

*   **Processing Time:** Depending on the complexity and amount, it can take anywhere from 5 to 15 working days.
*   **Bank Charges:** Similar to others, their charges hover around the 5% mark.
*   **Best For:** Individuals needing substantial amounts for student or tourist visas to strict destinations like the US, UK, or Canada.
